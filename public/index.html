Privacy and confidentiality notice

  • Do not speak, show or read out confidential or client-identifiable information (unpublished financials, PAN/Aadhaar/bank details, passwords, UPSI) while recording.
  • Video, audio and analysis stay in your browser; this page never uploads them. Two exceptions: (1) Chrome/Edge speech recognition sends your voice to the browser vendor's cloud service to produce the transcript; (2) the face-analysis model files are downloaded from public CDNs the first time.
  • If you record other people (for example trainees), take their informed consent and handle the files in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
