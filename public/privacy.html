SpeakWell - Privacy Notice

Last updated: 20 October 2026. This notice explains how Excel Vibez - CA Alok Sethi ("we", the Data Fiduciary) handles personal data when you use SpeakWell.

1. What we collect and why

DataPurposeBasis
Name, email, city, WhatsApp numberTo give you access, count users, and send service messages about SpeakWellYour consent (required checkbox)
Marketing opt-in (yes/no)To send tips, updates and offers about AI-in-finance training by WhatsApp/email, only if you ticked the optional boxYour separate, optional consent
Practice-session events: your registration ID, session duration, overall score, whether video was recordedTo measure how the app is used and improve itYour consent

We do not collect or receive your video, audio, transcript, report text or facial analysis. These stay in your browser and are saved only where you choose on your own device.

2. Third parties that process data

  • Vercel - hosts the website and the registration function.
  • Google (Sheets / Apps Script) - stores the registration records for us.
  • Speech recognition - when you record, Chrome or Edge sends your voice to the browser vendor's cloud service to create the transcript. This happens between your browser and that vendor, under its own policy.
  • MediaPipe model files are downloaded from public CDNs (jsDelivr, Google Cloud Storage); no personal data is sent by us.

These providers may process data on servers outside India. We do not sell your data.

3. How long we keep it

Registration data is kept for 24 months from your last activity, or until you withdraw consent or ask us to delete it, whichever is earlier, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

4. Your rights

You may, at any time: ask for a summary of the data we hold about you; correct or update it; withdraw consent (withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing, and we will stop the related processing and delete your data unless the law requires retention); ask for erasure; and nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated. Write to aloksethi.ai@gmail.com from the email address you registered with. We aim to respond within 15 days.

5. Grievance redressal

Contact: CA Alok Sethi, Grievance Officer, Excel Vibez, aloksethi.ai@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

6. Children

SpeakWell is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, tell us and we will delete the record.

7. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, the registration database is private and accessible only to the owner, and secrets are not stored in the public source code. No system is perfectly secure; if a personal data breach affects you we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as required by law.

8. Changes

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this notice and ask for your consent again where required.
